Stereo Camera Calibration (OpenCV format)
Source: matlab.mat, variable: stereoParams
Export method: MATLAB stereoParametersToOpenCV

Coordinate and unit conventions
- K: OpenCV camera matrix; principal point uses OpenCV pixel coordinates.
- D: OpenCV order [k1, k2, p1, p2, k3].
- R, T: camera 2 relative to camera 1.
- Image size: OpenCV order (width, height) = (1920, 1200).
- Translation/world units: millimeters. Depth computed with this calibration is therefore in millimeters unless converted.
